# Pomelo New Tab Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Pomelo New Tab processes browser tabs, bookmarks, browsing history, saved shortcuts, preferences, and per-domain usage-duration statistics solely to provide its new-tab workspace features.

All processed data remains on the user's device in Chrome's local extension storage. Pomelo does not transmit, sell, share, or use this data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Users can clear usage-duration statistics from Pomelo Settings. Removing the extension deletes extension-managed local data according to Chrome's storage behavior.

Pomelo does not execute remotely hosted code. Website favicons are obtained through Chrome's built-in favicon API.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support address shown on the Chrome Web Store listing.
